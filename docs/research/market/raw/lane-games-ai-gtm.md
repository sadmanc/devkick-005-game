# Sound Local: accent games, AI voice platforms and go-to-market benchmarks
Every source was accessed on 2026-11-15. Each figure has its URL in the two CSVs and in sources.md. Research stopped early at the lead's request, so gaps are marked **NOT RESEARCHED**.

## Key findings
1. **Nobody has a hit product where you speak a regional accent and get scored.** The accent games with traction are listening/guessing games:
   - Accentle: 126 accents, a paid pass and a pub licence.
   - DialectGuessr, AccentGuessr and Accent Guesser: free web toys.
   - Games where you perform an accent are judged by people: Heads Up! (it has an Accents deck), the Accentuate and Utter Nonsense board games.
   - The closest product to Sound Local is "Other Voices: Accent Game" on Play. You record an accent and get points for "accuracy and expression". It shows no rating and no download count.
   - Mimic Party on Steam already scores pitch and rhythm with a local AI, so that kind of scoring is not a moat on its own.
2. **People clearly want to watch accents.**
   - TikTok, from the Toklytics sample (a lower bound, because TikTok's own tag pages need a login): #accentchallenge 143.3M views across just 39 tracked videos; #accent 321.2M across 155.
   - YouTube: WIRED/Erik Singer's "How to Tell Accents Apart" has 3.4M views; Amy Walker "21 Accents" is a viral classic from 2008.
   - Creators: Kallmekris (25 accents in 5 minutes; about 32M followers per Matador); Tyler Collins, an Alaskan who does Scottish regional accents (30K+ followers, The Courier); Larry Dean, a Glasgow comedian (accent TikToks covered by Glasgow Live).
3. **Platform threat.**
   - **OpenAI, HIGH for casual use.** ChatGPT voice can already mimic and coach accents for free. What it lacks: game loop, calibrated scoring, characters and progression.
   - **Google, MEDIUM-HIGH.** Gemini Live has had accent/character switching since Nov 2025. Translate added Practice in 2025 and pronunciation practice in 2026, but it targets foreign-language learners.
   - **ElevenLabs, MEDIUM threat and a key supplier.** It has an accent-filterable voice library, Agents at $0.08/min plus LLM, and is worth $11B to $22B (raises dated 2026-02 and 2026-09). It sells infrastructure rather than consumer games.
   - **Azure, Speechace, SpeechSuper: suppliers.** Each scores against a standard US/UK/Indian English model, not Glaswegian. Azure costs about $1/hr. SpeechSuper has an Indian English model, which fits the planned Mumbai region.
   - **Voicemod, LOW.** It has not shipped an accent changer.
4. **Duolingo copy risk: HIGH if accents start trending.** Duolingo ships new verticals fast:
   - Chess reached 10M DAU.
   - It launched 148 AI-built courses in about a year.
   - Video Call with AI characters is now unlimited on Super.
   - It already runs Brawl Stars and Eggy Party partnerships.
   - Q3 2026: 64.9M DAU.

   Defence: be fun and regional ("same language, different voice") rather than a learning tool, and get to creators and the community first.
5. **Go-to-market: creators are the main growth channel for this category.**
   - BoldVoice: about $200 paid to one YouTube creator unlocked paid growth. It now has about $10M ARR and six-figure monthly paid UA across Meta, Google, TikTok and Apple Search Ads.
   - Praktika: one creator video took ARR from $1M to $12M in a month (RevenueCat).
   - Speak: a repeatable TikTok format reached 300M views (agency case study).
   - ELSA: creator programme paying 10% commission.
   - Duolingo: about 80% of users came by word of mouth (2023). TikTok organic reach is falling, so it is moving to a paid "creator army" (BI 2026-04).
6. **Costs (benchmarks are WEAK to MEDIUM).**
   - CPI: $1.5–3.5 iOS and $1.5–4 Android (Business of Apps, all apps); education about $2.91 (AppRevs, weak).
   - TikTok CPM: about $4 (ecommerce, 2025).
   - Education apps: 3.1% payer rate and $22.82 year-one LTV per payer (weak).
   - Creators: about $50–200 per video for nano accounts, about $350–850 at 5K–25K followers, $5K+ for macro (agency blogs, weak).

   At these numbers, paid installs only pay back for a high-converting subscription. Use creators and shareable clips first.
7. **Store and policy notes (partly researched).**
   - Apple 5.1.1: get consent for data collection and use clear purpose strings, which covers the microphone. The privacy policy must name any third parties that receive data (ElevenLabs, Anthropic) and explain retention and deletion.
   - Google Play AI-Generated Content policy applies (voice and text from an LLM), and the app must comply with Play's restricted-content rules.
   - Play Families policy: you must declare a target audience. If it includes children, you must use certified ad SDKs and cannot use personalised ads.
   - Most accent-game competitors are rated Everyone or 4+. Heads Up! on iOS is 13+.
   - **NOT RESEARCHED:** the exact COPPA 2025 rule text on voice recordings (the FTC page did not load), Apple's Kids Category specifics, Apple's AI guideline text, and the subscription guideline text (3.1.2).
