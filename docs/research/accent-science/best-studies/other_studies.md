# Best new accent-science studies for Sound Local

59 studies not already in `sources.json` (checked by DOI and title against all 113). Accessed 2026-11-19. Scope: accent distance, accent learning, Toronto/MTE, Glasgow/Scottish, Indian English, London/MLE, measurement robustness. General US/Canadian regional work (incl. Clopper & Pisoni, Canadian Shift) is left to the separate session.

**How to read it.** Titles, authors, years and venues come from Crossref/OpenAlex. Every DOI resolves; some publishers block automated checks, so open those links in a browser. "(abstract)" means the finding comes from the abstract. "(full text)" means it was checked in the PDF. **UNVERIFIED** marks any number or detail not checked. Evidence strength runs from 1 to 5: 5 = large and replicated, 4 = solid peer-reviewed empirical study or validation, 3 = smaller study, review or descriptive work.
