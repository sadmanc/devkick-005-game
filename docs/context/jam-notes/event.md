# The event: Devin Game Jam - Kick 005

Checked Nov 14, 2026 (Luma page, devkick.com, UTMIST and organizer LinkedIn posts).

## When and where
- Saturday **Nov 14, 2026**. Luma: 1-6pm. UTMIST's post said 12-7pm (older; trust Luma).
- University of Toronto, **Bahen Centre, BA1220**, 40 Willcocks St. Free. Lunch and snacks.

## Schedule (Luma)
| Time | Block |
| --- | --- |
| 1:00-2:00 | Set-up and networking (team forming for solos) |
| 2:00-4:30 | Building (2.5 hours) |
| 4:30-6:00 | Demos and judging |

## Who runs it
- Presented by **devkick** ("ship for kicks"), Toronto build community run by **Yohann Calpu** and
  **Jenny Calpu**. Yohann is a Devin Ambassador.
- Luma hosts: Yohann C, Jenny Calpu, Nader Dabit, Andi Bucescu, "partnerships UTMIST", Sayah.
- "Anchored by Cognition" (Devin's maker). Partners: UTMIST (University of Toronto Machine Intelligence
  Student Team), ElevenLabs, Meshy, Modal, Chroma Awards.
- One attendee's post: "hosted by devkick with Devin AI, MeshyAI, and Chroma Awards".

## Rules found
- "Build a game in one day and submit it to the Chroma Awards."
- Pick a Chroma Awards category; ship a playable game by the end of the night.
- Solo or teams; no max size stated. Come solo and find teammates at kickoff.
- For developers, designers, artists, audio people; no game-dev experience needed.
- UTMIST: university students and early-career developers, **19+**; applications screened.
- Devin credits provided. ElevenLabs (voice, music, SFX) and Meshy (3D) offered. Modal named by Yohann.

## Prize
- "The #1 team is fast-tracked to the semis of their chosen Chroma Awards category."
- No cash prize for the jam itself found.

## devkick culture (from devkick.com)
- "The bar is simple: demo or it didn't happen."
- Build nights: "no panels, no pitches"; you show work that runs, or say why it doesn't.
- People who ship consistently get first invites to "The Table": founder dinners, podcast tapings, real
  projects. Winning here is also a door into Toronto's Devin/AI builder circle.
