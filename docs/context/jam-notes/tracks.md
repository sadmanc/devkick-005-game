# What the hackathon wants: tracks, scoring, path to a prize

Checked Nov 14, 2026 (Luma, devkick.com, chromaawards.com prize pages + FAQ).

## In one paragraph
The jam has no tracks of its own. It is a one-day on-ramp into the **Chroma Awards Season 2 Game
division**. You pick one of Chroma's game categories (these are the "tracks"), build a **web game** for
it, demo it to the jam judges, and the jam's #1 team skips Chroma's early rounds and goes straight to
that category's **semifinals** (top 22). Everyone can still submit to Chroma normally until Jan 7, 2027.

## The tracks (Chroma game categories)
| Track | Brief, in their words | Prizes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Arcade & Party | "Fast, replayable, social games and party experiences that anyone can enjoy within seconds"; quick rounds, friendly competition, strong game feel | $2,000 / $1,000 / $500 |
| Puzzle & Strategy | "Clever systems", easy to learn, satisfying to master; goals clear without long tutorials | same |
| Music & Rhythm | "Sound as gameplay"; precise timing; audio and visuals respond clearly to the player | same |
| Interactive Narrative (Pickford) | Story, character, dialogue, meaningful choices that shape the story | same + Pickford grand prize |
| Experimental & Open Format | Unconventional mechanics, interfaces, formats; a clear creative idea | same |

Also open to the same game, with no extra entry:
- **Meshy Game of the Year** (gala trip + speech + 1 year Meshy) and **Meshy Awards** ($11,000, 11 places), if Meshy is used significantly.
- **ElevenLabs Awards** ($22,000, 22 places), if ElevenLabs is used significantly.
- Any other sponsor tool award you use (27 tools listed, e.g. Rosebud $500, Bolt, Luma, Hyper3D, SpriteCook).
- **Student Awards** ($55,000): submitters aged 18-24 who are students or young professionals can win paid
  Summer 2027 internships ($5,500 stipend). Worth checking if Sadman qualifies.
A **separate** entry: Behind-the-Scenes: Game Division (1-10 min making-of video), $3,500.

## How every entry is scored (official FAQ)
Four criteria, **equal weight**, each scored 1-10:
| Criterion | Weight | What it means for a game |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Theme & Division Purpose | 25% | Fits the chosen track's brief exactly |
| Creativity & AI Innovation | 25% | A fresh idea, and AI used in a new way (ideally in the gameplay) |
| Craft & Technical Polish | 25% | Game feel, mechanics, art direction, no bugs |
| Sound & Emotional Impact | 25% | Audio quality and whether it makes you feel something |
Category pages add: "judged on game feel, mechanics, art direction, audio, and overall polish".

## Rounds
Preliminary (pass/fail + score) -> Qualifiers (top 66%) -> Quarterfinals (top 50) -> **Semifinals (top
22)** -> Finals (up to 4 judges place the winners). Sponsor awards are judged separately by sponsor
judges. The jam win skips straight to the semifinals.

## Hard rules
- Web game, no download, **public** link that can be embedded in Chroma's gallery; plus a download link
  (gameplay video) and a thumbnail.
- English text or subtitles. 18+. Not from a sanctioned country.
- **No IP you don't own**: no other games' characters, brands, logos; no voice or face deepfakes of real
  people. Music must be yours, licensed, permitted, or fully AI-made.
- Nothing that wouldn't be allowed on YouTube (no graphic violence, explicit content, discrimination).
- Tool awards need the tool tag plus proof (short explanation, screenshot, or % estimate).
- Older projects are fine if never entered in an earlier season. Several entries allowed if each is
  substantially different; one project per category.
- You keep ownership; Chroma and sponsors may showcase it.

## The jam layer on top
- Jam judges (devkick hosts, Cognition and partners) pick #1 at the 4:30-6pm demos. No published criteria;
  the culture is "demo or it didn't happen". Devin is the sponsor's tool, so visible Devin use matters here
  even though Chroma itself doesn't score Devin.
