# Chroma Awards, Season 2 (where the winner goes)

Checked Nov 14, 2026 on chromaawards.com prize pages and the ElevenLabs announcement.

## What it is
- AI film, music video, ad and **game** competition. Started 2023 as Project Odyssey. Incubated by
  **ElevenLabs**; backed by CapCut, Cantina, Runware and 100+ others.
- Season 1 (Aug-Nov 2025, on Devpost): 6,500+ submissions, about $191,500 paid.
- Season 2: submissions **Oct 19, 2026 to Jan 7, 2027** (11:45pm PT). Winners on a livestream
  **Feb 11, 2027**. Gala in April 2027. Any project made after Feb 1, 2026 qualifies.
- Season 2 is "doubling down on Games". Game division total: $20,500.

## Game categories (each: 1st $2,000, 2nd $1,000, 3rd $500)
| Category | What its page asks for |
| --- | --- |
| Arcade & Party | "Easy to pick up", anyone enjoys it within seconds; fast, replayable, social; quick rounds, friendly competition, strong game feel |
| Experimental & Open Format | Unconventional mechanics, interfaces, formats; "clear intent" even when abstract |
| Interactive Narrative (Pickford) | Story, character, dialogue, meaningful choices; decisions visibly shape the story. 1st also gets a Pickford grand prize |
| Music & Rhythm | "Sound as gameplay"; precise timing; audio and visuals respond clearly to the player |
| Puzzle & Strategy | Clever systems, easy to learn and satisfying to master; goals clear without long tutorials |

Every category is also "judged on **game feel, mechanics, art direction, audio, and overall polish**".

## Extra prizes one game can win at the same time (automatic, by tagging tools + proof)
- **Meshy Game of the Year**: flight + hotel + VIP gala invite + acceptance speech + 1 year of Meshy.
  Any game entry that uses Meshy "in a significant way".
- **Meshy Awards**: $11,000 across 11 places (1st $4,000), any division.
- **ElevenLabs Awards**: $22,000 across 22 places (1st $6,000 + gala trip + creator program spot).
- **Rosebud Awards**: $500.
- **Behind-the-Scenes: Game Division** (a separate 1-10 min video on how it was made): $3,500 pool,
  judged on clarity, editing, and how well the process is shown (prompts, tools, iteration, human direction).

## Hard rules for a game entry
- Playable on the **web, no download**, at a **public** link (not unlisted, private or password-locked).
- Include a **gameplay video** download link.
- In-game text in English (or English subtitles).
- One category per project.
- Submitter 18+, not from a sanctioned country.
- Original work; only code, art, music, IP and likenesses you have permission for. So: **no copied games
  or characters** here, unlike his Roblox rule.
- "Human-led storytelling and respectful, authentic representation"; zero tolerance for discrimination.

## How it's judged
- Official FAQ: four equal criteria scored 1-10: Theme & Division Purpose, Creativity & AI Innovation,
  Craft & Technical Polish, Sound & Emotional Impact. Full rounds in tracks.md.
- Five rounds per the FAQ (prelim, qualifiers, quarters, semis top 22, finals with up to 4 judges); the
  ElevenLabs post said "three rounds", so trust the FAQ. Judges have traditional creative and AI backgrounds.
- ElevenLabs' line: is it moving, is it well made, does it sound right. **"AI is not why a project wins."**
- Season 2 judges: "announced soon". Past judges: Davis Chang (Chroma), Justin Hackney (Wonder Studios),
  Matt Wolfe (Future Tools), Fred Grinstein (Machine Cinema).
- Season 1 game winners: not found in text form (only in ceremony videos on YouTube).
